Engaged Views = views × Wilson-adjusted engagement rate — an estimate of how many views represented real engagement rather than a passive replay, not a metric YouTube itself publishes. Success Score (0–100) = 55% age-adjusted view velocity (views ÷ days live, with the age floored at 3 days so a brand-new video's opening-day spike — nearly every video gets one — can't inflate its rate, and an old video isn't rewarded just for having had longer to accumulate raw views) + 45% that same Wilson-adjusted engagement (likes+comments vs. views, penalised for small sample sizes so a couple of superfan likes on a low-view clip can't outrank real traction). Ranked against the other 19 videos shown, not an absolute scale. YouTube's public data has no watch-time, audience-retention, unique-viewer, or day-by-day view history — only a live cumulative total — so this is the fairest comparison that data allows, not a perfect one.
